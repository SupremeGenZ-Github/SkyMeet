# Third-party assets

Virtual backgrounds use @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.32 (Apache-2.0), including its WASM runtime copied from the installed package, and Google's MediaPipe Selfie Segmenter float16 version 1 model.

Model source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_segmenter/float16/1/selfie_segmenter.tflite
Documentation: https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter
Package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mediapipe/tasks-vision

Other dependency licenses remain with their respective npm packages. No third-party sound recordings are included; short effects are synthesized with Web Audio.
